Everything you need before you fly

Flights & airport transfers

If your flight was booked by our travel coordinator, keep your e-ticket accessible offline — you may be asked to show your return ticket on arrival. All tickets are also saved in the shared Google Drive folder.

Airport transfers to the hotel are arranged for everyone. Look for a representative holding a TAXDOME sign in the arrivals area. Return transfers are arranged based on your flight time.

Arrival: Sep 20, 2026 · Departure: Sep 26, 2026

Arriving at a different time, or have specific needs? Contact your travel coordinator to arrange an individual transfer.
